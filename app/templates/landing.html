What is the difference between memoization and tabulation in dynamic programming?
CampusX AI
Memoization is a top-down approach: you solve the problem recursively and cache results as you go. It only computes subproblems you actually need.

Tabulation is bottom-up: you build a table of all subproblems from the smallest case upward, iteratively. It avoids recursion overhead and is often more memory-efficient.
