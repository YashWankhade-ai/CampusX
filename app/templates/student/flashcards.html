Master Theorem Recurrence

What are the 3 cases of T(n) = aT(n/b) + f(n)?

(Click to flip card →)
ANSWER REVEALED

Compare f(n) to n^(log_b a):
Case 1: f(n) = O(n^(log_b a - ε)) → Θ(n^log_b a)
Case 2: f(n) = Θ(n^log_b a) → Θ(n^log_b a • log n)
Case 3: f(n) = Ω(n^(log_b a + ε)) → Θ(f(n))
